# World Graph Studio Code of Conduct

> Build Your Story Once. Create Everywhere.

## Our Commitment

World Graph Studio is committed to fostering an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and respectful community.

We want contributors, storytellers, filmmakers, developers, educators, researchers, artists, and users to feel safe participating in project discussions and activities.

We are committed to creating an environment where everyone can collaborate constructively regardless of experience level, background, identity, skill set, or area of expertise.

---

# Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Be constructive when providing feedback.
- Assume positive intent.
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Support collaboration and knowledge sharing.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Personal attacks.
- Discrimination or exclusionary behavior.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Threatening language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Bad-faith participation.

---

# Community Standards

When disagreements occur:

- Discuss ideas, not people.
- Focus on facts and project goals.
- Remain respectful.
- Seek collaborative solutions.
- Escalate concerns through maintainers when necessary.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories
- Discussions
- Pull requests
- Issues
- Documentation
- Community meetings
- Project communication channels
- Events conducted under the World Graph Studio name

---

# Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. Document the incident.
2. Contact a project maintainer.
3. Provide relevant details and context.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially whenever possible.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including:

- Providing guidance or warnings
- Removing inappropriate content
- Limiting participation
- Temporarily suspending access
- Permanently removing access to project communities

Enforcement decisions will be made in the best interest of maintaining a healthy and productive community.

---

# Project Values

World Graph Studio is built upon the following values:

- Open Source Collaboration
- Creator Ownership
- Open Standards
- Transparency
- Inclusivity
- Education
- Community Growth
- Long-Term Sustainability

---

# A Shared Responsibility

Creating a healthy community is everyone's responsibility.

Every contributor helps shape the culture of World Graph Studio.

We encourage all participants to model respectful, collaborative, and constructive behavior.

---

# Final Statement

World Graph Studio exists to advance storytelling through open technology and community collaboration.

By participating in this project, you agree to uphold these standards and help create a welcoming environment for everyone.
