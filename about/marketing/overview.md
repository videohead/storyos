World Graph Studio is a free, open-source, self-hosted creative production platform for worldbuilding, story analysis, asset generation, and production planning. It's intended for creators who are filmmakers, game creators, scriptwriters, and video producers.

Built on the world's most popular content management platform and powered by open-source AI, World Graph Studio helps creators build connected worlds.

Generate worlds, stories, ideas, images, videos, films, and 3D content using local and open AI models without subscriptions, censors, usage quotas, or commercial AI credits.
