## FADE IN:

### EXT. VILLAGE COTTAGE - MORNING

A small cottage sits at the edge of a quiet woodland village.

LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD stands near the doorway wearing a distinctive red cloak.

GRANDMOTHER prepares a basket of food.

**GRANDMOTHER**

Please take this basket through the forest.

**LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD**

I will deliver it safely.

Grandmother hands over the basket.

Red nods and starts down the path leading toward the woods.

### EXT. FOREST PATH - DAY

Tall trees line a winding path.

Little Red Riding Hood walks through the forest carrying the basket.

A WOLF steps onto the path ahead.

**WOLF**

Where are you going today?

**LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD**

I am visiting my grandmother.

The Wolf studies the basket.

**WOLF**

How interesting.

The Wolf watches as Red continues down the path.
